Miso Garlic Noodle

Description

This quick 15-minute miso garlic ramen delivers a rich, savory broth packed with umami flavor. Chewy noodles swim in a fragrant garlic and ginger base, topped with a jammy soft-boiled egg, tender greens, and crispy scallions for an effortless, comforting meal at home.

Ingredients

  • Ramen noodles: 2 packs of instant ramen noodles (discard the seasoning packets) or 2 portions of fresh ramen noodles
  • Broth: 4 cups (960ml) chicken or vegetable broth
  • Aromatics: 3 cloves garlic (minced) and 1 teaspoon fresh ginger (grated)
  • Soy sauce: 1 tablespoon low-sodium soy sauce
  • Sesame oil: 1 teaspoon toasted sesame oil
  • Oil for cooking: 1 tablespoon vegetable or neutral oil
  • Greens: 2 heads baby bok choy (halved) or 1 cup fresh spinach
  • Toppings: 2 soft-boiled eggs (halved), 2 sliced green onions, toasted sesame seeds, and chili oil (optional)

Steps

  1. Bring a small pot of water to a gentle boil. Carefully lower in the eggs and cook for exactly 6.5 minutes for a jammy yolk, then transfer them immediately to an ice bath to cool before peeling.
  2. Heat the neutral oil in a medium pot over medium heat. Add the minced garlic and grated ginger, cooking for 1 minute until fragrant without letting them burn.
  3. Pour in the broth and soy sauce, bringing the liquid to a gentle simmer.
  4. Scoop a ladleful of hot broth into a small bowl and whisk in the miso paste until smooth. Stir the dissolved miso mixture and toasted sesame oil back into the pot, keeping the heat low so the miso does not boil.
  5. Add the ramen noodles and baby bok choy directly into the simmering broth. Cook for 2 to 3 minutes until the noodles are tender and the greens are bright and wilted.
  6. 1. Divide the noodles, greens, and hot broth between two bowls. Top each bowl with a halved soft-boiled egg, sliced green onions, sesame seeds, and a drizzle of chili oil.
